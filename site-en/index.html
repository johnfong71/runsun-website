Enterprise Sovereign AI

The elasticity of cloud infrastructure. The control of enterprise-dedicated AI.

The Elasticity of Public Cloud. The Sovereignty of Private AI.

Enterprise Sovereign AI enables organizations to harness the power of public AI while building, controlling, and continuously improving their own proprietary intelligence.

At the center is the Proprietary AI Factory: enterprise-dedicated GPU infrastructure, professionally managed with an agreed path to expand capacity. It gives organizations more deployment control than a typical shared service without requiring them to build and operate an on-premises cluster.

This architecture powers two complementary Data Flywheels:

Enterprise Data Flywheel

Curated business data, application feedback and evaluation results can improve enterprise-owned models through controlled post-training, versioning and release checks. Improvement is measured rather than assumed.

Public Data Flywheel

Enterprises can use advances in public AI models and optionally contribute approved, privacy-reviewed information where provider terms explicitly permit. Ordinary inference calls do not automatically train public models.

Together, the two loops connect broad public intelligence with enterprise-specific models, protected knowledge and dedicated AI capacity.

Industries

Infrastructure designed for demanding AI workloads across industries. The examples below describe supported use cases, not necessarily customer deployments.

AI Startups

For AI R&D enterprises and product teams

For an AI startup, the training calendar is the product roadmap: pre-training runs, fine-tuning cycles and evaluation jobs decide how fast the next model ships. RunSun gives small teams cluster-scale compute without buying hardware — scale up for a training run, hold steady inference capacity for launch, and develop the application itself on the same platform.

Smart City

For government and related enterprises

City systems generate inference work that never sleeps: camera networks to analyze, IoT telemetry to process, traffic to understand in real time. RunSun GPU capacity powers video and image analysis, IoT and data analytics, and smart-transportation workloads — for governments and the enterprises that build for them.

Manufacturing

For production and manufacturing enterprises

Factories are becoming simulation problems: a digital twin of the line, vision models inspecting every unit, logistics that re-plan themselves. Manufacturers run digital-twin simulation, industrial and quality inspection, intelligent logistics and equipment-maintenance analytics on RunSun — compute-heavy workloads that reward GPU throughput.

Digital Platforms

For e-commerce, gaming and content platforms

Consumer internet runs on models that retrain constantly and media that renders on demand: recommendation engines, digital humans, virtual worlds. E-commerce, gaming and content platforms run AI-powered recommendation, digital-human generation and metaverse rendering on RunSun — workloads where demand spikes are the normal case.

Finance

For financial institutions, securities firms, private equity and investment funds

Financial workloads split two ways: research that eats compute — quantitative analysis, backtesting, investment algorithm research — and production systems that cannot blink, like fraud detection and risk control scoring transactions in real time. Institutions run both on RunSun, inside the isolated environments sensitive data requires.

Education

For universities and research institutions

Universities need two kinds of compute: teaching capacity that flexes with the semester, and research capacity that can chase a deadline. RunSun supports AI teaching and hands-on practice, plus research in deep learning, accelerated computing and data science — from a single course lab to institute-scale clusters.

Design & Generative AI

For advertisers and architectural design institutes

Design work is rendering work: scenes, walkthroughs and generated concepts that must come back fast enough to iterate on. Advertisers and architectural design institutes use RunSun for graphics rendering, AIGC design platforms and GPU-desktop VDI, so distributed teams share one high-end workstation pool.

Life Sciences

For pharmaceutical and biological enterprises

Modern biology is a compute pipeline: sequencing runs to align, protein structures to fold in simulation, molecule libraries to screen before anything reaches a lab bench. Pharmaceutical and biological enterprises run gene-sequencing analysis, protein-folding simulation and small-molecule screening on RunSun's GPU clusters.

RunSun AIDC Platform — full stack

Four infrastructure-to-workload layers. RunSun delivers the GPU and infrastructure foundation; higher-layer software responsibilities depend on the customer deployment and service agreement.

Read it bottom-up. IaaS is the physical foundation — GPU clusters, bare metal and tiered file, object and vector storage. PaaS keeps it running: the AI cluster with Kubernetes and HPC scheduling, the operator fleet and the compiler toolchain, plus billing, IAM and monitoring. MaaS puts models to work — model and workflow management over the mainstream open-source frameworks. SaaS is where it lands: the industry and commercial applications your users actually touch. One stack, managed as one platform, from a single node to thousands.
